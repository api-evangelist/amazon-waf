Amazon Cognito's infrastructure modernization enables high-throughput performance supporting millions of users and thousands of transactions per second, customer-managed encryption keys via AWS KMS, and multi-Region replication for business continuity. The migration used shadow mode validation and dual-write architecture to achieve zero downtime while migrating hundreds of millions of user profiles.
