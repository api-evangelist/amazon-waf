AWS now releases its Spring 2026 SOC 1 and 2 compliance reports in machine-readable OSCAL format alongside the PDF versions, covering 188 services over a 12-month period. This makes AWS the first major cloud provider to offer these reports in NIST's open OSCAL standard format.
